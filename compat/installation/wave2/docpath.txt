After installation, the LCOV documentation can be found in both ``man`` and
``html`` formats.

  - **man**:
       $ export MANPATH=$MANPATH:$LCOV_HOME/share/lcov/man
       $ man lcov # or 'man genhtml', 'man llvm2lcov', etc.

  - **html**
       point your browser to ``$LCOV_HOME/share/lcov/html/index.html``

To run the LCOV regression test suite on your installation::

    $ cp -r $LCOV_HOME/share/test path/to/myTestDir
    $ cd path/to/myTestDir
    $ make [COVERAGE=1]
